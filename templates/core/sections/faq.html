Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your adventure.

How do I book an adventure?

Browse our adventures, choose your preferred trip, and submit a request with your preferred date and group size. Our team will confirm availability and arrangements directly.

What's included in the package?

Most packages include accommodation, transportation, permits, guides and meals as specified.

Can I cancel my booking?

Pending requests can be cancelled from My Bookings before departure. Confirmed requests can be cancelled online at least 15 days before departure. Contact our team for changes closer to your trip.

Are your guides licensed?

Absolutely. Every guide is government licensed and highly experienced.
